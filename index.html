ServiceNow Scoped Application

Visitor Management
System.

A centralized visitor management solution built on ServiceNow to simplify visitor registration, streamline host approvals, and manage on-site check-in and check-out operations.

01 / The Challenge

Making visitor operations more organized.

Visitor handling involves collecting accurate visitor details, identifying the employee being visited, obtaining approval, and tracking entry and exit. When these activities are handled separately or manually, it becomes harder to maintain a consistent record and visibility into visit status.

This project addresses that need with a structured workflow inside the ServiceNow platform, connecting registration, host approval, and receptionist actions in one portal experience.

02 / Application Modules

One portal. Three key views.

A unified interface designed around the visitor lifecycle and the responsibilities of the people involved.

MODULE 01

Dashboard & Reports

Provides a quick view of visitor activity, including total visits, pending approvals, today's visits, checked-in visitors, completed visits, and rejected requests.

MODULE 02

Visitor Registration

Captures visitor identity and contact details, visitor type, company, host employee, visit purpose, category, and scheduled date/time.

MODULE 03

Receptionist

Enables reception staff to review visitor records and perform permitted check-in and check-out actions from the portal interface.

04 / Process Flow

From registration to checkout.

The approval and visit lifecycle is coordinated through ServiceNow automation and controlled status transitions.

RegisterVisitor details and visit information are submitted.
Request ApprovalFlow Designer routes the request to the selected host.
Host DecisionApproval moves the visit to Scheduled; rejection marks it Rejected.
Check InReception records the visitor's arrival.
Check OutReception records the end of the visit.
TrackDashboard summaries reflect visit statuses.
